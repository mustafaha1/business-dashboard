How to Use This Dashboard

🕒 Staff Time Clock

  • Select a staff member from the dropdown
  • Enter their hourly rate
  • Click "Clock In" to start tracking time
  • Multiple staff can be clocked in simultaneously
  • Each staff member gets their own "Clock Out" button
  • Hours are automatically added to staff assignments when clocking out

👤 Individual Accounts

  • Select "Staff Account" or "Supplier Account"
  • Choose the specific staff member or supplier
  • Set the date range for the account period
  • Click "Load Account" to view detailed breakdown
  • See total amounts owed and detailed transaction history
  • Share account statements with staff/suppliers

📊 Sales Data Entry

  • Select your date range using the date pickers
  • Enter daily sales figures for Shop, Delivery, Online, and Card Payment
  • Click "Save" for each day to store your data
  • Click "Generate Report" to update all tables

👥 Staff Management

  • Add staff members using the Staff Management section
  • Assign staff to specific dates with hours worked and hourly rate
  • Different staff can have different rates, even on different days
  • Staff costs are automatically calculated and included in expenses

📦 Supplier Management

  • Add suppliers using the Supplier Management section
  • Record supplier orders with amounts for specific dates
  • Supplier costs are automatically totaled and included in expenses

💰 Profit Sharing

  • Add partner email addresses to share profits with
  • Set the percentage of profit to share with partners
  • Profit is automatically divided equally among all partners
  • The remaining profit goes to the owner

📤 Sharing & Exporting

  • Use the Share Report button to export your data
  • Choose between Email, WhatsApp, or Download options
  • Download saves as a text file that you can save anywhere
  • All data is saved in the backend database
